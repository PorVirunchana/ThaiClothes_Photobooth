**Thai Clothes Photobooth**

A web-based interactive photobooth created as part of the Thai Wisdom course to present the beauty and cultural identity of Thai traditional clothing in a way that feels engaging and relatable to Gen Z.

**🌐 Live Demo**

https://thai-clothes-photobooth.vercel.app/

**🎯 Project Purpose**

The project aims to introduce Thai traditional clothing (ชุดไทย) through a modern and interactive experience rather than presenting the topic only through conventional slides or written content.

The concept combines:

🇹🇭 Thai cultural identity --- introducing the beauty and characteristics of Thai traditional clothing

✨ Gen Z-friendly presentation --- using a fun, visual, and interactive format to make the topic more engaging

📸 Photobooth experience --- giving users something to interact with while learning about Thai clothing

🌟 Celebrity inspiration --- featuring well-known celebrities wearing Thai traditional clothing to connect the cultural topic with contemporary popular culture and attract attention

**✨ Features**

Browse and select Thai-themed photo frames

Interactive photobooth experience

Thai traditional clothing-inspired visual designs

Celebrity-inspired content featuring Thai traditional clothing

Simple and engaging interface designed for a younger audience

**💡 Concept**

The photobooth was designed as an interactive element alongside a presentation about Thai traditional clothing. Instead of simply explaining the history and characteristics of Thai clothing, the project adds a playful activity that allows the audience to engage with the topic.

By combining Thai cultural content + contemporary celebrity references + a photobooth experience, the project aims to make traditional culture feel more approachable and interesting to a Gen Z audience.

**🚀 Deployment**

The project is deployed on Vercel.
